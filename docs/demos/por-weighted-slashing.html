What this proves

The scenario proves deterministic PoR checkpoint penalty replay and matching durable results under controlled finalized evidence. The live panel proves agreement among shadow observers only when its status feed is present. It does not demonstrate equivocation injected into a live cluster, validator-side PoR weight enforcement, or a host-chain slash deploy.
