A SHORT FIELD GUIDE

Three things to watch.

A stable chart is useful evidence.
It is not the whole story.

01

Reputation & ranking

Ratings update each validator’s score. Look for stable scores and ordering over several rounds, not just a flat moment.

02

Weight & exclusion

Consensus uses active reputation. A slash ejects the key; retained capital never appears in its voting share. Remaining shares can rise without scores increasing.

03

Agreement & progress

Nodes should agree at the same checkpoint. After a restart, new blocks must finalize too. Agreement on an old state does not prove recovery.
